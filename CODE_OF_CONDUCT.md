# Trussium Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free
community for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or
contribution type.

## Expected behavior

Contributors and maintainers are expected to:

- communicate constructively and respectfully;
- welcome differing viewpoints and good-faith feedback;
- focus discussion on technical merit and the project’s shared goals;
- respect privacy and obtain consent before sharing personal information; and
- acknowledge mistakes and help resolve disagreements productively.

## Unacceptable behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks,
threats, intimidation, deliberate disruption, sexualized language or imagery,
and publishing private information without permission.

## Scope and enforcement

This Code applies in project repositories, issue trackers, pull requests, and
other project spaces. Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits,
issues, and contributions that do not align with it, and may take proportionate
enforcement action.

## Reporting concerns

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainers listed in
[MAINTAINERS.md](MAINTAINERS.md). Reports will be handled as confidentially as
practicable.
